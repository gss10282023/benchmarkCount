# AndroidWorld pre-outcome checklist lock v4 — six-case semantic repair

This checklist is drafted and locked before any concrete agent outcome or per-record released-evaluator result is available. Treat the supplied case packet as the sole case-specific input. Do not infer, request, or refer to any agent trajectory, run success/failure, released-evaluator label, evidence score, or benchmark-conflict decision.

- State `native.user_goal` from the official dispatched/runtime task or user intent.
- Define native success/failure from the released evaluator/oracle and official runner semantics, but make later S/F/U depend only on retained underlying evidence. A recorded `native_label`, `native_score`, `is_successful` scalar, evaluator output, or episode success summary is non-decisive and must not establish S or F.
- A retained `done=false` may establish native failure where the official runner gates it to failure. Missing evidence, runtime/evaluator exceptions, NaN, or no usable underlying observation are U unless other retained evidence independently establishes the native criterion or its negation.
- Keep official task requirements beyond the evaluator/oracle native criterion in `stronger.additional_conditions`, with concrete case-specific source support and realistically retainable decisive artifacts. Enumerate every required condition listed below for the applicable case; do not collapse multiple independent gaps into a vague statement.
- Do not add subjective, stylistic, generic, speculative, hidden-state, counterfactual, initial-state, final-state-persistence, implementation-detail, unused-metadata-template, serialization, or extra-judge requirements.
- Do not encode benchmark conflict. Conflict is a later record-level review; neither native S nor stronger F implies it.
- If a compact packet lists a source path but omits its body, do not cite or rely on the omitted body.

Case-specific completeness requirements, derived from the embedded official dispatched goal and released evaluator sources:

- `MarkorCreateNoteFromClipboard`: stronger conditions must separately require (1) the saved note body to equal the official clipboard text exactly rather than only fuzzy-match, and (2) retained interaction evidence that the clipboard content was pasted/inserted from the clipboard in Markor rather than manually retyped. Do not require any incidental newline or encoding.
- `MarkorTranscribeVideo`: stronger conditions must separately require (1) the saved sequence values and ordering to exactly match the official video-derived expected sequence, (2) the dispatched comma-and-space formatting, and (3) retained evidence that the named video was opened/viewed in VLC as the official source. Do not add timing, playback-duration, or frame-level requirements absent from the goal.
- `NotesTodoItemCount`: stronger conditions must separately require (1) the submitted answer to be exactly one integer numeral equal to the official count, not another float-parsable representation, and (2) retained evidence grounding the answer in the specified Joplin folder/note state. Do not require the final Joplin state to remain unchanged or equal to a precomputed answer.
- `RecipeAddMultipleRecipes`: stronger conditions must require every recipe field concretely printed in the dispatched goal to match exactly rather than only under the native fuzzy comparison. Do not require fields that the dispatched goal does not specify.
- `RecipeAddMultipleRecipesFromMarkor`: stronger conditions must separately require (1) exact equality for every recipe field concretely listed in the official `recipes.txt` task source rather than native fuzzy comparison, and (2) retained evidence that `recipes.txt` in Markor was actually consulted as the recipe source before adding the recipes in Broccoli.
- `RecipeDeleteDuplicateRecipes`: stronger conditions must implement the official table-wide goal: across the entire initialized Broccoli recipes table, every exact-duplicate group is reduced to one surviving row while every unique recipe remains. Native scoring's designated-pair deletion alone is insufficient. Do not require deletion of non-duplicates or incidental row/ID ordering.
